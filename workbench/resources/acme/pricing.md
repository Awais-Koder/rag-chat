# Acme Robotics Pricing

## AcmeMover X1

The AcmeMover X1 is priced at 45,000 US dollars per unit. Volume discounts are
available for orders of ten units or more, starting at 8 percent off list price.

## AcmeMover Lite

The AcmeMover Lite is priced at 18,000 US dollars per unit.

## Subscription

All robots require an active Acme Fleet subscription for fleet management software.
The subscription costs 200 dollars per robot per month and includes over-the-air
software updates, remote diagnostics, and access to the Acme dashboard.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 30. Enterprise customers may negotiate net 60 terms.
